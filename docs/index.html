HHFinAi / Sustainable finance / 0.2.0

Human-Rights and Social-Value-Chain Diligence Agent

Institutional-quality buy-side research, designed to support tradable investment decisions through a traceable, auditable workflow.

The decision question

Who may be harmed across the value chain, how severe is the risk, and what prevention, remedy and investment responses are evidenced?

Research outputs

  • Affected-stakeholder and value-chain map
  • Salient-harm and evidence register
  • Prevention/remedy milestones and coverage
  • Separate financial-transmission and stewardship handoffs
Control boundaries

Risks to people are not reduced to monetary loss or a supplier-spend score. Allegations, company responses and adjudicated findings remain distinct. No personal victim data should be published in public repositories. No independent certification, embedded LLM, live feeds or automatic trading.
